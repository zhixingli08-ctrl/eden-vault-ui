03 / Plot planet

2,000 plots,
minted by burning, soulbound

Plots can only be obtained by burning tokens. They are permanently bound to the burning address and cannot be transferred or sold. Each burn is split greedily, highest tier first: Common 100,000 (weight 1.0), Rare 200,000 (2.2), Epic 300,000 (3.5). Genesis lasts 30 minutes at up to 20 plots per minute (max 600), then issuance continues to 2,000. The plot pool runs 30-minute rounds, releasing 80% evenly over the minutes, 20% rolling over, split by activated weight. Every 10 minutes you only need to burn 1,000 tokens per 1.0 weight to activate; in a quiet window a single activated holder takes that period's whole dividend. Plot dividends never expire; unclaimed prize-round rewards expire after 30 days.

04 / Floor redemption

Vault floor,
redeemed by weight

Floor redemption needs no activation. Your quota = weight × quota per weight. The cap per 1.0 weight = max(100,000, ⌈2 × 15,000,000 ÷ network issued weight⌉), recomputed live, rounded down per address. 80% of the floor tax goes to the current tier, 20% to the next; a filled tier opens the next one at once. Redeemed tokens are 100% burned and do not count toward the prize pool, plot minting, or activation. The floor is not a guarantee or a promise to pay.
